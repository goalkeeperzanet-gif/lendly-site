What we collect

  • Account info: your email address and a password (stored as a one-way hash, never in plain text).
  • Items you log: item name, a photo you choose to add, who you lent it to, their contact info, estimated value, notes, and dates — all entered by you.
  • Payment confirmation: when you pay for access, Stripe (our payment processor) tells us the email address used to pay, so we can match it to your account. We never see or store your card details — Stripe handles that entirely.

What we don't collect

We don't track you across other websites, sell your data, or show ads. We don't collect your card number, bank info, or physical location.

Where your data lives

Account and payment-status data is stored with Supabase, a database provider. Your lent-item details (photos, notes, etc.) are stored locally in your own browser on each device you use — they are not synced to our servers unless stated otherwise.

How we use it

  • To let you sign in and recognize your account.
  • To check whether you've paid for access.
  • To let the app function (showing you the items you've logged).

We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

Payments

Payments are processed entirely by Stripe, Inc. Stripe's own privacy policy governs how they handle your payment details.
